Title: Why I Renamed My Multi-Agent System

Abstract:

The first version of my architecture used a familiar pattern in AI projects: a mythology-inspired naming scheme. Expert personas were "Gods." Execution workers were "Dwarves." The names were memorable, but they were also misleading.

As the design matured, it became clear that the system was not really about powerful entities issuing commands. It was about communities of expertise collaborating to solve problems and commission work.

The resulting vocabulary—The Collegium, Fellows, Foundries, Forges, Commissions, and Artifacts—better reflects the architecture itself. The names describe the roles participants play rather than the mythology they evoke.

This article explores how naming influences software architecture, why metaphors matter, and how choosing language that reflects system behavior can make complex designs easier to explain, teach, and evolve.
