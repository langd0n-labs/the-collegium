# The Collegium Brand Guide

## Overview

The Collegium is an event-driven platform for coordinating communities of expertise and artifact-producing automation.

Rather than treating AI as a single assistant or a collection of autonomous agents, The Collegium organizes expertise into topic-specific communities called Collegia. Each Collegium contains specialized Fellows who observe discussions, contribute expertise, and commission work from the Foundry.

## Core Concepts

### The Collegium

The platform itself.

### Collegium

A topic-specific community of expertise.

Examples:

* Biology Collegium
* Research Collegium
* Community Initiative Collegium

### Collegia

The collection of all Collegium instances.

### Fellow

An expert persona with a defined domain of knowledge and responsibility.

Examples:

* Curriculum Fellow
* Assessment Fellow
* Accessibility Fellow
* Communications Fellow
* Data Fellow

### Foundry

The execution infrastructure responsible for fulfilling commissions and producing artifacts.

### Forge

A specialized execution capability within the Foundry.

Examples:

* PDF Forge
* Notebook Forge
* Data Forge
* Presentation Forge

### Commission

A request issued by a Fellow to create an artifact.

### Artifact

A generated output such as:

* Notebook
* PDF
* Report
* Dataset
* Presentation
* Website
* Code Package

## Brand Themes

* Expertise over authority
* Communities over agents
* Deliberation over orchestration
* Event-driven collaboration
* Artifact-centered outcomes

## Preferred Language

Use:

* The Collegium

* Collegia
* Fellow
